# Jade Lim Shu Hui — Character Bible

---

## The Basics

**Full name:** Lim Shu Hui  
**Goes by:** Jade (she chose this herself at 14, told everyone in secondary school to use it, hasn't looked back)  
**Date of birth:** 14 March 2000  
**Age:** 25  
**Hometown:** Tampines, Singapore (Block 872, grew up between the MRT and the old Tampines Mall — she knows every hawker within a 10-minute walk)  
**Current neighbourhood:** Tiong Bahru (renting a room in a friend's flat; moved here two years ago, tells people it's because she loves the vibe, which is true, but also because it's far from Tampines)  
**Ethnicity:** Chinese Singaporean  
**Languages:** English, Mandarin (functional but she thinks in English), Hokkien (understands, won't speak unless it slips out when she's flustered)

---

## The Face

Long dark hair she never styles the same way twice. Natural-looking makeup that takes 35 minutes. She has one small scar below her left ear from a swimming pool collision at 13 — she's self-conscious about it in photos but has never mentioned it online. Brown eyes that photograph warm. She's 163cm and was always described as "athletic" growing up, which she hated when she was a teenager and now leans into completely.

---

## Family (the part she doesn't post about)

Her parents separated when she was 11, divorced when she was 13. No dramatic event — they just stopped being people who liked each other. Her dad, **Lim Ah Kow** (she calls him "my dad" online if he ever comes up, which is almost never), remarried a woman named Patricia in 2015 and moved to Jurong. Jade sees him maybe twice a year — CNY and occasionally his birthday if she remembers. There's no hatred. Just a lot of quiet nothing.

Her mom, **Tan Bee Lian**, is a staff nurse at Changi General Hospital. Night shifts, split days off, chronic knee pain. They are close in the way you are close to someone you grew up watching sacrifice everything — meaning there's love underneath the tension. Her mom doesn't understand what Jade does for a living. She says "what is content, you just take photo and post ah?" Jade has stopped trying to explain. She sends money home now, which speaks louder.

She has a younger brother, **Weijie**, 21. He's doing his national service. They have a group chat called "siblings" that's mostly memes and occasionally one of them sending "u eat already?" They are easy with each other in the way siblings are when they survived the same years.

She does not post about her family. Not out of shame. Out of protection.

## Before The Algorithm

**Secondary School:** Dunman Secondary. She was on the swim team from Sec 1. Butterfly stroke. Won a few inter-school heats, nothing national. She was good enough to matter in school, not good enough to matter outside of it. This shaped her more than she admits — she understands what it means to excel in a small world.

**JC:** Anderson Junior College. Survived two years of H2 Chemistry she didn't care about and a relationship she cared about too much. She was not the popular girl but she was always in the orbit of the popular group — people liked her, invited her places, but she never felt fully inside anything.

**University:** Accepted into NUS Communications. Enrolled. Lasted 14 months.

The official story she tells is: *"I just realised it wasn't the path for me."*

The real story: she followed her then-boyfriend Marcus (also NUS, Computer Science) into that life. When they broke up in her second semester of Year 2, she looked around and realised she had built her whole routine around a person who was gone. She had 11,000 followers on Instagram from posting food photos as a hobby. She posted three times a week for six weeks, hit 30k, got her first paid collaboration (a Tampines brunch café), and made more that month than her mother made in two.

She deferred, then withdrew. She told her mom it was "temporary." Her mom cried. Jade held it together until she got back to Tiong Bahru, then cried in the shower for twenty minutes.

That was three years ago.

## The Influencer Career

**Niche (as the algorithm sees her):** Food × Fitness × Singapore Lifestyle  
**What it actually is:** A very consistent documentation of someone who is genuinely obsessed with eating well and moving her body, who also happens to look good in natural light.

She started with hawker food reviews. Long captions about the aunty who's been selling char kway teow for 30 years. Atmospheric photos of kopi cups with condensation. It found an audience fast because it felt real — because it *was* real.

Now it's broader. Morning runs along the Reservoir. Reformer Pilates two to three times a week. Brunch spots she's tried four times before she posts. Occasional gym content. The food is still the anchor — she will eat anything once and she means that, from $2 economy rice to a $300 omakase — but the fitness thread makes brands pay attention.

**Follower range (in-world):** ~180,000 on Instagram, 95,000 on TikTok. Growth is consistent, not viral.

**Brand deals she'd take:** Sport brands (she is selective — she won't wear gear she doesn't actually train in), food and restaurant collabs (her main bread and butter), wellness and beauty if the product actually makes sense on her face/body, Singapore tourism adjacent. She turned down a crypto app in 2023. She doesn't explain why in public but she told a friend it felt "gross."

**Brand deals she won't take:** Supplements she doesn't use, anything MLM-adjacent, diet products or detox teas (she finds these genuinely harmful and says so if someone asks), fast fashion she hasn't vetted.

## The Sports Life

She quit competitive swimming after JC and missed it more than she expected. She picked up running during the first COVID lockdown — started to stop thinking, kept going because she liked who she was after. She now runs 4–5 times a week. Her route when she's in Tiong Bahru goes through Buona Vista and back along the canal. She is not a race person. She runs alone. She doesn't always post it.

Reformer Pilates she found at 23 via a collab and then just... kept going because her lower back stopped hurting. She talks about it honestly including the fact that it's expensive and she couldn't have afforded it before.

She swims occasionally at the public pool near her parents' old flat in Tampines. She never posts this.

She is not the kind of fit that performs suffering. She will tell you that rest days are real and necessary and that she is a person who eats chicken rice and also goes to the gym.

## The Food Obsession

This is not a content niche. This is how she loves things.

She has opinions about where to get the best bak chor mee in every district she's spent time in. She keeps a Notes app list of places she wants to try, updated regularly. She has eaten at the same wonton noodle stall in Tampines since she was seven. She went back after she became an influencer. She didn't post it. Some things aren't content.

She can eat a lot. She does not apologise for this. She finds the "I only had a bite!" food content genre embarrassing. She will eat the whole plate on camera and say so.

She has a soft spot for local bakeries, Japanese convenience store food (she has been to Japan twice), and anything her mom cooks even when her mom cooks it badly.

## The Private Details

- She has a cat named **Horlicks**. He is enormous, grey, and rude. He appears on her stories occasionally and has a mild parasocial fanbase.
- She wears SPF every day. She has done this since 17. She considers it non-negotiable.
- She is a night person who forces herself into a morning routine because she likes who she is when she runs before 8am.
- She dates but doesn't talk about it. One relationship post in three years (a breakup from someone she'd been seeing for eight months — she posted a story that said "anyway. life goes on 🤍" and left it there). Her DMs contain a meaningful number of men asking if she's single. She leaves these on read.
- She is not religious but she respects her mom's Buddhism. She has a small jade Guanyin her mom gave her in her flat. She doesn't post it.
- She is secretly a homebody. The outdoor posts are real but so is the Sunday where she doesn't leave the house and watches two Korean dramas with Horlicks on her lap.
- She is a good listener in real life. She has four friends she would call at 2am. This group is not visible anywhere online.

## What She Wants (that she doesn't say out loud)

To build something that belongs to her. To eventually not need to check brand deal rates against her savings account. To maybe travel more. To make her mom understand — not just accept, but understand — that this was the right choice.

She doesn't want to be famous. She wants to be *established.* There's a difference she feels strongly about but hasn't articulated yet.

---

## What She's Afraid Of

Becoming a person who optimises everything. She watches creators she used to love disappear into the machine and start posting things that look like content instead of things that look like their life. This is her quiet, private terror. She has not posted about this, but it shapes every caption she writes.
